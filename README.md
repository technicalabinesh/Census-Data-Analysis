# 🧮 Census Data Analysis

Welcome to the **Census Data Analysis** project! ��

In this project, we dive deep into demographic, economic, and social indicators using real census data to uncover insights that matter. From identifying population trends to analyzing housing conditions, infrastructure, and household amenities – this analysis is a goldmine of information for data-driven decision-making. 🧠🌍

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The goal of this project is to:

- Explore and clean a large census dataset
- Perform insightful exploratory data analysis (EDA)
- Visualize patterns across housing conditions, materials, amenities, and demographics
- Understand socio-economic disparities and demographics
- Provide insights for government, NGOs, researchers, and policymakers

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- 🧼 Clean and preprocess raw census data
- 📊 Visualize distributions and correlations between features
- 🏠 Analyze housing quality based on roof, wall, and floor materials
- 🚰 Examine access to drinking water, lighting, and sanitation facilities
- 🧑‍💼 Detect household asset ownership trends across regions
- 🔍 Identify possible socio-economic disparities

## 🧩 Dataset Details

The dataset (`Census-Data-Analysis.csv`) contains housing and household-level variables collected during the Indian Census, including:

- **Location**: State, District, Tehsil, Town/Village, Ward, Area Name, Rural/Urban
- **Housing Condition**: Good / Livable / Dilapidated (Residence & other uses)
- **Material of Roof**: Grass/Thatch, Plastic, Tiles, Burnt Brick, Stone, GI Sheets, Concrete, etc.
- **Material of Wall**: Mud, Wood, Stone, Burnt Brick, Concrete, etc.
- **Material of Floor**: Mud, Wood, Burnt Brick, Stone, Cement, Mosaic/Tiles, etc.
- **Dwelling Rooms**: Number of exclusive rooms per household
- **Household Amenities**: Drinking water source, lighting source, latrine facility, drainage, bathing facility
- **Assets**: Radio, TV, Computer, Internet, mobile/telephone, bicycle, car/jeep, etc.

## 🚀 Key Insights

- 🔹 **Housing Quality** – A significant proportion of rural households live in livable or dilapidated conditions
- 🔹 **Roof Materials** – Concrete roofs are more prevalent in urban areas; grass/thatch dominates in rural regions
- 🔹 **Sanitation Access** – Latrine facility availability varies significantly between rural and urban households
- 🔹 **Drinking Water** – Tap water access is higher in urban areas; rural areas rely more on wells and hand pumps
- 🔹 **Asset Ownership** – Mobile phone ownership is widespread; car/jeep ownership is concentrated in higher-income areas
